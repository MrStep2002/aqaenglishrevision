Who am I?

In short, I'm a teacher who got very bored during lockdown. 

2020 has been tough for so many reasons, and I'm one of those people who gets super fidgety if I don't have a project, so alongside learning to meditate and finally using the weights I bought myself almost ten years ago I thought it might be fun to build a website to help my year 11s with their revision. 

But, true to form, I've turned a simple task into some kind of Herculean mission and ended up with this. What started as a way to support year 11 with An Inspector Calls has turned into an entire course online...

I am aware that at the moment there are pictures and text being used on the site that I do not own the rights to, and will work to fix this in the near future. If you would like me to remove anything, please get in touch and I will get on it straight away.

Please also be aware that this is a not-for-profit enterprise and so please don't sue!!
