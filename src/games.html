Games

A Christmas Carol

Marley’s ChaseHunt for missing words in a snowy London maze while Marley’s cash boxes give chase. Fog RunJump for ten word wreaths on the streets of London to reach Fred’s Christmas party. Quote RecallChoose or type the missing words from quotations. Quote BuilderRebuild a quotation by choosing its words and phrases in order. Context QuizTest your knowledge of Dickens and Victorian society. Plot OrderPut events from the novella in order. Plot QuizAnswer multiple-choice questions about events across all five staves. Word FormationChange each supplied word into the form the sentence needs. Word in ContextChoose the word that completes a sentence about the novella. HangmanGuess the letters of a word or short quote before the mouse is lost. CrosswordFill the grid from clues about the novella’s language. WordsearchFind eight Level 1 words hidden in a new 10 × 10 grid. Drag across each word to collect it. Connecting WallFind four groups of four among words, characters and objects from the novella. Essay JigsawArrange the sentences to build a coherent paragraph. Extract ChallengeSelect two phrases from an extract and check how well they answer the question. How long to earn it?Explore the scale of wealth by comparing how long it takes to earn different amounts.
