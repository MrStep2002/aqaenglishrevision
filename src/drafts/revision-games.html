WordWall Revision Games 

WordWall is a website that offers educational games that can be embedded into websites. I've added a selection of the games below, though there are more.

The deal is:

  • You pay a fixed fee (£6 a month per account) and can have a maximum of 34 games with that
  • You can make extra games but only if you delete an old one
  • If you stop paying the fees the games are still usable, though you can't make any more-  so we could make 34 and then stop paying and keep the games on the site
  • There are school rates, but I don't think it's worth it as we just want them for revision purposes - if we build them correctly we don't really need to worry about the results, it's more being able to offer good quality revision games to the kids
  • There are LOADS of other quizzes on the site, though it's very similar to TES in that most of them are a bit of a mess

I've currently put some of them embedded into the An Inspector Calls pages, and honestly think that 34 will be plenty for a site like this - there's only four topics, if you include unseen poetry (and I'm not sure how they'd be used on that anyway!)

Included below are:

  • A plot ordering activity - very simple plot reminder
  • A true of false game for An Inspector Calls where statements fly by at speed and the student needs to say True or False as many times as possible in a minute
  • A quotes matching exercise that forces  students to see each quote quite a few times, and eventually they have to learn them to win the game
  • A Christmas Carol Cloze quotes exercise 
  • A version of Whack-a-Mole I made to remind students of keywords for Mr Birling (they see each word a hundred times in the course of the game - plus, it's whack-a-mole and who doesn't love that!!!)
  • A version of Hangman that I've setup for quotes from An Inspector Calls
  • A Crossword - (and I know it's not a reason to celebrate, but I love crosswords)
  • An ordering activity - who said what
  • And, at the bottom, two quizzes from other teachers on the site - and these DO NOT count towards our 34 games total
