Writing Skills

I wish there was a magic formula for teaching spelling and grammar but there really isn't. Spelling and grammar is the intellectual equivalent of weights, shuttle runs or press-ups. Learning it isn't fun, but it works your brain and stops you looking like an idiot in front of people who care about this kind of thing. 

And on that note, if you want a job that requires you to email anyone, or write a report, or write a marketing email or a sales pitch, you're going to have to know your their from your there from your they're or you'll look like an idiot. And the worst thing is that you'll look like an idiot without even knowing what you've done wrong.

I tried building help into this site, but in the end decided I was better off using the right tool for the right job. As a result, there's a Google Classroom that has a TONNE of useful resources - (and before I get comments, a tonne is the correct spelling for the metric measurement. Ton is the Imperial spelling of the weight. I go metric, myself, since it's a little more scientific and I'd bet it's the future. We can only hope that imperialism has had its day.)
