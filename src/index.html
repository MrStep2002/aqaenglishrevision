Welcome to my site! I'm an English teacher who originally put this together to support his students during lockdown, and recently started messing around with Ai as a way to improve the experience.

There are some games on here, but the site is mostly text based. That's a warning and a reassurance. If you're going to stay ahead in this world, you need to read. It's exercise for the mind and it's good for you.

I've tried to make the experience of revising as painless as possible but getting a good mark at GCSE requires work and that's a good thing. You should work, you should train your mind to analyse and understand complex ideas, you should increase your vocabulary and expand your horizons.

To get started, simply choose one of the texts or exams below and get going. Or you can read more about the site by clicking here...
