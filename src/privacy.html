Privacy and visitor statistics

Last updated: 21 November 2026.

Understanding which resources are useful

We use GoatCounter to see how often the site is visited and which pages are used. We use these combined statistics to improve the revision resources.

The reports include page addresses, referring websites, browser and device information, and approximate country. We do not send game answers or contact form responses to GoatCounter, and we do not use these statistics to build student profiles or for advertising.

GoatCounter does not use analytics cookies. It temporarily uses IP addresses and browser information to recognise repeat visits for up to eight hours; IP addresses are not saved in its statistics database. Read GoatCounter's privacy policy for details.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics is an additional, optional way to understand visits, page use and engagement. It loads only if you choose to allow it. Declining it does not affect access to the resources or games.

When allowed, it uses first-party analytics cookies, including _ga and a cookie whose name starts with _ga_, to distinguish browsers and visits. These cookies last up to 180 days. The setup disables Google advertising features and does not send game answers or contact form responses as analytics events. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your Google Analytics choice is remembered in this browser for 180 days. You can change it here at any time. Withdrawing permission stops Google Analytics and removes its analytics cookies from this site.

Your choice for both services

You can exclude future visits from this browser using the option below. You can change your mind here at any time.

This choice is saved on this browser only, using local storage. If you clear the site's saved data, the choice resets. The rest of the site still works when you exclude your visits.

Other features

Reading settings and the pages you mark as read are saved in your browser. They are not sent to either analytics service.

Some pages load fonts, videos, documents or a contact form from Google services. Those services have their own privacy policy; the analytics choices above control GoatCounter and Google Analytics, rather than those embedded services.

For questions about the site or these statistics, please use the contact form.
