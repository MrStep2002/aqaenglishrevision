An Inspector Calls
Games

You'll find a range of games below that should help you prepare for the big day. 

BUT PLEASE REMEMBER: you're not being tested on your ability to play Mr Birling Whack-a-Mole so use these to help you remember the key quotes, key words or key facts, but don't pretend that playing them is all there is to revision!

AND ALSO REMEMBER: You're not expected to know all the quotes at all, but if you play the quotes games and encounter a quote you don't know, don't worry: play the game, get the quote, and be confident that when it comes to the exam you'll be in a better position because you'll be more familiar with even more quotes...
