In a nutshell

Basically, An Inspector Calls is a super easy play....

A family are celebrating their daughter marrying someone really posh. Then a police inspector turns up and it turns out that they've all been involved in the death of this girl. By the end of the play, the audience is quite sure that that every member of the cast is guilty, while the cast are still unsure.
