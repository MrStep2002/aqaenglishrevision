Unseen Poetry

Start with what you notice

Read the poem once to get your bearings. Who is speaking? What are they describing, and how do they seem to feel about it? Read it again and look for a word, image or change in tone that helped you decide.

Analyse and compare

One poem

Make a point about the poem, quote a short phrase and explain what its words suggest. Look for a shift between the start and the end, especially if it changes how you understand the speaker.

Two poems

Choose something both poems explore. Compare how each poet presents it, using a detail from each poem to show how their choices differ or overlap.
