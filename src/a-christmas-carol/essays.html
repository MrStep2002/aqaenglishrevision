Read like a writer

Good writing starts with good reading.

One of the best ways to learn to write an essay is to read a few.

See how another writer makes a point, chooses a quotation and turns it into an argument. Read once for the ideas, then take another look at how the essay works.

There’s more than one way to write a good essay. Read these, argue with them, and borrow a useful move or two for your own writing.
